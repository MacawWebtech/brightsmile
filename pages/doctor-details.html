Clinical Background

A Passion for Biomimetic Smile Artistry

Dr. Sarah Mitchell is an internationally recognized authority in minimally invasive porcelain veneers, non-prep ceramic facings, and digital smile architecture.

After graduating Magna Cum Laude from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine, Dr. Mitchell completed advanced hospital residency at NYU Langone Health. She earned her prestigious Fellowship in the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (FAACD)—a distinction held by fewer than 1% of dentists worldwide.

Her clinical philosophy blends biological tooth preservation with mathematical smile harmonization. Using 3D photogrammetry and Digital Smile Design, Dr. Mitchell allows patients to "test drive" their restored smile before any tooth preparation occurs.
